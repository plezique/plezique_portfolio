Merch Design

Bicol University Merchandise

Merchandise design for Bicol University.

Client Bicol University - University Student Council
Year Sept 2024 - May 2025
Role Merchandise Designer

Project Overview

This project marked my first major undertaking as a graphic designer, representing a significant milestone in my career. The collection included a diverse range of merchandise items:

  • T-shirts
  • Stickers
  • Pins
  • Lanyards
  • Tote bags
  • Hoodies
  • Varsity jackets
  • Caps

This was the first time in my career that I created such a comprehensive array of different merchandise types, allowing me to explore and expand various aspects of my creative capabilities. The project provided invaluable experience in understanding the unique design considerations required for different product formats and materials, ultimately contributing to my growth and development as both an artist and a designer.

Tools Used

Adobe Illustrator Adobe Photoshop Figma
