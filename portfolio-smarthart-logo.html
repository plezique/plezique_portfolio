Logo Design

SmartHart Solutions LLC Logo

Logo designs for SmartHart Solutions LLC.

Client Andrew Hartweg
Year July 2025
Role Logo Designer

Project Overview

This project was commissioned by the same client responsible for the book cover and illustrations work. The designs represent proposed logos for SmartHart Solutions LLC, though they were ultimately not selected for use by the company. Despite not being implemented, this project served as a valuable entry point into logo design, introducing me to the principles and challenges of creating brand identities. The experience provided important foundational knowledge in understanding brand representation, visual identity systems, and the strategic thinking required in logo design.
