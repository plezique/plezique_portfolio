Project Overview

I began creating digital art at the age of 13, though I didn't fully commit to it until I was 16. Starting in 2020, I dedicated myself to these digital artworks, which have played a crucial role throughout my artistic journey. I started sharing my work on my art account on Instagram, where you can explore my collection of artworks and witness my development as an artist. The account serves as a visual diary of my creative evolution, showcasing both technical growth and the various themes and styles I've explored over the years.
