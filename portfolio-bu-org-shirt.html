Bicol University Organization Shirts

Polo shirt design for various Bicol University organizations.

Client Bicol University Organizations
Year March 2025
Role Shirt Designer

Project Overview

This project involved designing polo shirts for various organizations within Bicol University. Each design needed to represent the unique identity of the respective organization while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic that reflects the university's overall brand. The designs were created to be both functional and visually appealing, suitable for various events and activities.
