Bicol University Polangui - College Student Council

Publication materials for Bicol University Polangui - College Student Council.

Client Bicol University Polangui
Timeline August 2023 - May 2024
Role Co-Leader of Creatives Committee

Project Overview

I applied for the Bicol University Polangui - College Student Council for the Creatives Committee as a freshman in 2023, marking an important step in my creative career. This role allowed me to demonstrate my ability to translate conceptual ideas into compelling publication materials that were utilized across the organization's platforms. Through creating various graphic design assets, I developed stronger technical skills and gained exposure to new facets of digital art, significantly contributing to my growth as both an artist and a designer.
